OVERVIEW

Crestwood issues physical (paper) titles for all passenger motor vehicles. Customers
from Crestwood will have a paper title document to present. Crestwood has been a
recognized paper-title state in Marion's Admin. Rule 4 list since 2009.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

TITLE SYSTEM: PAPER

Crestwood paper title characteristics:
• Physical title document issued by the Crestwood Department of Motor Vehicles.
• Title is mailed to the vehicle owner, or to the lienholder if a lien is active.
• Title face shows: owner name(s), vehicle description (VIN, year, make, model), title
  number (format: CST-XXXXXXXX), issue date, and lienholder information if applicable.
• Assignment section on the reverse side of the title. The seller completes the
  assignment section; buyer signs as buyer. All fields must be completed in ink.

If a lien is active: The title is held by the lienholder (not mailed to the owner)
until the lien is paid in full. The owner holds a lien notification letter from
Crestwood DMV but not the title itself.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

LIEN PROCEDURES IN CRESTWOOD

Crestwood lien release options:

Option 1 — Endorsement on title face (most common):
The lienholder stamps or writes a release endorsement on the title face, signed by an
authorized officer of the lienholder. The endorsement typically reads:
"Lien released. [Lienholder name]. [Date]. Signed: ___________"
The lienholder then mails the released title to the owner.

Option 2 — Separate Form CST-LR (Crestwood Lien Release):
Some Crestwood lenders issue a separate lien release letter using Crestwood Form CST-LR,
on lienholder letterhead, referencing the vehicle VIN and stating the lien is released.
Marion DMV accepts this form as an alternative to the endorsement. (See Procedure Manual
§ 4.2 Item 8(b) for Marion's requirements on separate lien release letters.)

Crestwood does NOT have a Marion-equivalent registered lender system; Form TR-3 is
not typically used for Crestwood lien releases.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

EMISSIONS PROGRAM

Crestwood operates a state emissions testing program for vehicles registered in
Crestwood's designated metro counties.

IMPORTANT: A Crestwood emissions test certificate is NOT accepted by Marion DMV.
Marion requires a Marion-authorized emissions test. See Admin. Rule 2.4.

Crestwood uses the term "Reconstructed" for vehicles rebuilt from salvage — this is
the same concept as Marion's "Reconstructed" brand (MVC § 310(2)).

Note: Crestwood has separate "Salvage" and "Reconstructed" brands. These are two
distinct designations in Crestwood; they appear separately on the title and are treated
separately on the Marion title.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

SALES TAX

Crestwood state sales tax rate on vehicle transfers: 6.0%.

Marion has a reciprocity agreement with Crestwood. Customers receive credit for Crestwood
tax paid, up to the Marion tax owed. See Admin. Rule 7 and Marion Tax Reciprocity Schedule.

Documentation of Crestwood tax paid: typically the original bill of sale with the tax
line itemized, or a Crestwood tax receipt. The exact dollar amount of Crestwood tax paid
must be verified; examiners may not estimate it.
