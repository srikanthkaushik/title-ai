Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State of Marion, in session assembled, that the
following provisions governing the titling and registration of motor vehicles previously titled
in jurisdictions outside the State of Marion are hereby adopted and shall be construed in
accordance with the purposes of this Chapter:

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Sec. 301. JURISDICTION AND APPLICABILITY.

(a) Any motor vehicle operated upon the public roads, highways, or other public ways of the
State of Marion must bear a valid Marion title and registration, except as otherwise provided
in this Chapter or by rule of the Administrator.

(b) The provisions of this Part apply to any motor vehicle:
    (1) previously titled in a state, territory, district, or possession of the United States
        other than Marion; and
    (2) which is to be operated in Marion by an owner who has established Marion residency or
        who has purchased the vehicle in a transaction occurring in or incident to Marion.

(c) Motorcycles, commercial trucks with a gross vehicle weight rating exceeding 26,000 pounds,
and certain specialty vehicles are subject to separate titling requirements under Chapter 4 of
this Code and are not governed by §§ 301–305.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Sec. 302. OBLIGATION TO OBTAIN MARION TITLE.

(a) An owner of a motor vehicle previously titled in another jurisdiction who establishes
Marion residency shall apply for a Marion title and registration within thirty (30) days of
establishing such residency.

(b) An owner who purchases a motor vehicle titled in another jurisdiction, where the purchase
occurs in Marion or the vehicle is to be registered in Marion, shall apply for a Marion title
within thirty (30) days of the date of purchase.

(c) "Establish Marion residency" for purposes of this section means the execution of a lease
or purchase agreement for Marion real property, the commencement of Marion employment, or
other affirmative act demonstrating intent to make Marion the owner's principal domicile, as
further defined by administrative rule.

(d) Failure to apply within the period prescribed by subsection (a) or (b) shall result in
a late penalty as prescribed in Admin. Rule 9. The Administrator may waive the penalty upon
good cause shown.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Sec. 303. REQUIRED INSTRUMENTS FOR TRANSFER.

(a) An applicant for an out-of-state title transfer shall present to the Department, at a
Marion DMV office or authorized agent, the following instruments, including but not limited
to:

    (1) The original title issued by the origin jurisdiction, properly assigned to the
        applicant, with all intervening assignments complete; or, in the case of a vehicle
        from a jurisdiction participating in electronic lien and title systems, a confirmed
        electronic release as prescribed by administrative rule;

    (2) A completed Marion Application for Out-of-State Title Transfer (Form TR-1), executed
        and signed by the applicant;

    (3) Proof of valid motor vehicle insurance meeting Marion's minimum coverage requirements,
        in the applicant's name and covering the subject vehicle;

    (4) Payment of all applicable fees as set forth in Admin. Rule 9, and payment of all
        sales and use tax as prescribed in §§ 320–322 and Admin. Rule 7.

(b) The Administrator may by rule prescribe additional instruments or documentation required
for specific categories of transfer, including but not limited to:
    — vehicles subject to lien (see § 304);
    — vehicles bearing a title brand (see § 305 and §§ 310–312);
    — vehicles from jurisdictions using electronic lien and title systems (see Admin. Rule 2.1,
      §§ 2.1.3–2.1.4).

(c) No Marion title shall be issued until all required instruments have been received, verified,
and accepted by a Marion DMV examiner or supervisor.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Sec. 304. LIENS.

(a) No Marion title shall be issued for a motor vehicle while an unsatisfied lien is recorded
on the origin title, or while an unsatisfied lien is reflected in an electronic lien and title
system of the origin jurisdiction, unless:

    (1) a complete and authentic lien release, satisfying the requirements of Admin. Rule 2.1,
        accompanies the application; or

    (2) the lienholder has submitted an electronic lien release through such systems as the
        Administrator may designate pursuant to rule.

(b) The Administrator shall prescribe by rule (see Admin. Rule 2.1, §§ 2.1.3–2.1.4, ELT
procedures) the procedures applicable to vehicles from jurisdictions participating in
electronic lien and title programs, including the form and sufficiency of electronic lien
releases.

(c) Where a lien cannot be confirmed as satisfied, the Administrator or a designated
supervisor shall review the matter before the applicant is advised of document requirements
or fee obligations. Examiners shall not communicate document requirements to an applicant in
a pending lien-exception situation without supervisor authorization.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Sec. 305. TITLE BRANDS.

(a) A Marion title issued for a vehicle bearing a brand on the origin title shall carry such
brand in perpetuity. No Marion title may be issued that omits a brand shown on the origin
title.

(b) Where the brand terminology used by the origin jurisdiction differs from Marion's brand
nomenclature established under §§ 310–312, the brand shall be converted to the Marion
equivalent as specified in the Administrator's Brand Equivalency Schedule, promulgated
pursuant to § 312.

(c) Vehicles bearing a title brand from the origin jurisdiction, whether or not the brand
term appears in Marion's Brand Equivalency Schedule, are subject to supervisor review
before the Marion title is issued. See §§ 310–312 and Admin. Rule 5 for applicable
procedures.

(d) The presence of a brand does not, by itself, preclude issuance of a Marion title;
however, no Marion title for a branded vehicle shall be issued without supervisor
clearance as specified in Admin. Rule 5.
