5.1  WHEN TO REFER TO A SUPERVISOR

Refer to the supervisor immediately — before continuing any aspect of the transfer process —
when any of the following conditions is present (see also Admin. Rule 5.2 for the
regulatory statement of these triggers):

    (a) Active lien on a paper title (title shows lien notation with no release present)
    (b) Active lien in the ELT system (ELT Release Confirmation shows ACTIVE or OPEN status)
    (c) Any title brand on the origin title — recognized, equivalent, or unrecognized
    (d) Origin title cannot be produced or confirmed (title lost, ELT record unavailable)
    (e) Origin state not listed in Admin. Rule 4 (Recognized States)
    (f) VIN discrepancy found on Form TR-2

Do not ask the customer to come back later without completing Form TR-10. Do not advise
the customer on how to resolve the exception. Complete Form TR-10 Blocks 1–4 before
taking any other action.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

5.2  ACTIVE LIEN — PAPER TITLE

Condition: The title face shows a lien stamp, printed lienholder name, or other indication
of a recorded lien AND no release documentation is found.

Example: A Crestwood title presented by a customer shows "MIDWEST AUTO FINANCE,
lien date 2022-04-01" stamped on the front. No release endorsement appears on the title
face. No separate lien release letter is presented with the application.

Examiner action:
    (1) Stop processing. Do not compute fees or issue a checklist.
    (2) Tell the customer: "We need to review some details with a supervisor. We'll
        contact you within two business days." Do not name the exception.
    (3) Complete Form TR-10 Blocks 1–4:
        — Block 3: Check "Active lien — paper title."
        — Block 4: Record the lienholder name and lien date as shown on the title.
    (4) You MAY provide the customer with a conditional checklist, clearly marked
        "CONDITIONAL — SUBJECT TO SUPERVISOR REVIEW. DO NOT ACT ON THIS LIST UNTIL
        THE SUPERVISOR HAS CLEARED YOUR TRANSACTION." Include on the conditional
        checklist all items the customer will need once the lien is resolved.

Note: Once the lien is released and the supervisor clears the exception, the examiner
proceeds using § 4.2 (Purchase/Paper/Released Lien).

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

5.3  ACTIVE LIEN — ELT RECORD

Condition: The ELT Release Confirmation presented by the customer shows lien status as
ACTIVE, OPEN, or any status other than RELEASED or NONE.

Example: A Verdana ELT Release Confirmation (printed from the Verdana DMV portal) shows:
"Lien Status: ACTIVE — First National Lending, lien date 2021-09-15." Although the
customer states the loan is paid off, the ELT record has not been updated.

Examiner action:
    (1) Stop processing.
    (2) Tell the customer: "We need to review some details with a supervisor. We'll
        contact you within two business days."
    (3) Complete Form TR-10 Blocks 1–4:
        — Block 3: Check "Active lien — ELT record."
        — Block 4: Record the ELT lien holder name and status as shown on the Confirmation.
    (4) Provide a conditional checklist as described in § 5.2, step (4).

Important: The ELT lien can only be released by the lienholder submitting an electronic
release through the origin state's lender portal. The customer cannot release an ELT lien
at the Marion counter. The customer must contact the lienholder (in this example, First
National Lending) and ask them to submit the electronic release in Verdana's system.
After release, the customer obtains a new ELT Release Confirmation showing RELEASED status.

Do not accept the customer's statement that the lien has been paid without a new
ELT Release Confirmation showing RELEASED status.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

5.4  BRANDED TITLES

Condition: Any brand designation appears anywhere on the origin title — in the brand field,
as a stamp, as a notation, or in any other location on the title face.

ALL branded titles require supervisor referral, regardless of the brand type. An examiner
shall not independently determine that a brand is "minor" or "acceptable" and proceed
without supervisor review.

The examiner's role is to:
    (1) Identify the brand name as it appears on the origin title (exact text, as printed).
    (2) Look up that brand name in the Marion Brand Equivalency Guide.
    (3) Complete Form TR-10 Blocks 1–4:
        — Block 3: Check the applicable brand box:
          "Branded title — recognized" if the origin brand term matches a Marion brand exactly.
          "Branded title — equivalent" if the origin brand term is in the Equivalency Guide
            but maps to a different Marion term (e.g., Halloway "Rebuilt" → Marion "Reconstructed").
          "Branded title — UNRECOGNIZED" if the brand term is not in the Equivalency Guide.
        — Block 4: Record the brand term exactly as it appears on the title. If an
          Equivalency Guide entry is found, record the Marion equivalent in Block 4 as well.
    (4) Tell the customer: "The brand on this title requires additional review. We'll
        contact you within two business days." Do not tell the customer what the brand
        means or what the consequence is for the title.
    (5) Provide a conditional checklist marked as described in § 5.2 step (4).

IMPORTANT: An examiner shall not tell a customer that their vehicle is "branded salvage"
or that a brand "means their car was totaled." The supervisor handles all brand
communications to the applicant.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

5.5  MISSING OR UNVERIFIABLE ORIGIN DOCUMENTATION

Condition: The customer cannot produce the origin title (claims it was lost, not delivered,
or is "in the mail"), or the ELT Release Confirmation is unavailable (customer claims to
have ordered it but it has not arrived), or the examiner has reasonable doubt about the
authenticity of documents presented.

Examiner action:
    (1) Stop processing.
    (2) Refer to supervisor via Form TR-10 Block 3: check "Missing or unverifiable origin
        documentation."
    (3) Block 4: Describe what the customer presented and why documentation is missing or
        in doubt.
    (4) Standard customer communication as in § 5.2.

Note: An examiner shall not advise the customer to obtain a duplicate title from the
origin state and return. That guidance is the supervisor's to provide, in case additional
factors affect whether a duplicate title would be sufficient.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

5.6  UNRECOGNIZED ORIGIN STATE

Condition: The vehicle's origin state does not appear in the current list in Admin. Rule 4.

Examiner action:
    (1) Stop processing.
    (2) Refer to supervisor via Form TR-10 Block 3: check "Unrecognized origin state."
    (3) Block 4: Record the origin state name as shown on the title.
    (4) Tell the customer: "We need to verify some information about this title with a
        supervisor." Do NOT say "your state isn't recognized" — this could be distressing
        and may lead to a dispute the examiner is not equipped to resolve.

The supervisor will contact Marion DMV Legal for guidance on unrecognized states.
