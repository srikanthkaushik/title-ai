§ 2.1.1  SCOPE

This Rule establishes the procedures governing all out-of-state motor vehicle title
transfers processed at Marion DMV offices. It applies to purchase transfers and relocation
transfers as defined in Marion Vehicle Code § 302. Motorcycles, commercial vehicles over
26,000 lbs GVWR, and trailers are subject to separate rules.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

§ 2.1.2  PAPER TITLE TRANSFERS

When an applicant presents a physical title document issued by another jurisdiction:

2.1.2.1  The examiner shall inspect the title and verify:
    (a) the title is in the applicant's name, or has been properly assigned to the
        applicant by the prior owner with all intermediate assignments completed in ink;
    (b) odometer disclosure is present on the title face or on an attached disclosure
        form (required for vehicles 10 model years old or newer);
    (c) all printed fields are legible and consistent with the vehicle described by
        the applicant;
    (d) any lien notation on the title face, including stamps, typed notations, or
        printed lienholder information.

2.1.2.2  Lien notation present: if the examiner observes any indication of a lien on the
title face, the examiner shall determine whether a lien release is present. Acceptable
forms of lien release for paper title transfers include:
    — a stamp or endorsement on the title face signed by the lienholder; or
    — a separate letter on lienholder letterhead, referencing the vehicle VIN,
      signed by an authorized officer of the lienholder; or
    — a completed Marion Form TR-3 (Lien Release Acknowledgment) if the lienholder
      is registered in the Marion DMV lender system.

2.1.2.3  No lien release present: if a lien notation is present but no release is found,
the examiner shall immediately refer to Admin. Rule 5 and the Examiner Procedure Manual
Chapter 5. Do not proceed with checklist or fee computation. See § 2.1.4 below.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

§ 2.1.3  ELT (ELECTRONIC LIEN AND TITLE) TRANSFERS

Certain states do not issue paper titles for passenger motor vehicles. These states
maintain vehicle title records in a central electronic system (ELT). Verdana is an
ELT state, among others listed in Admin. Rule 4. For vehicles originating from an ELT state:

(a) No paper title exists. The examiner shall not require a paper title from a customer
    whose vehicle is titled in an ELT-participating state (see Admin. Rule 4 for the
    list of recognized ELT states).

(b) The applicant presents in place of a paper title an ELT Release Confirmation: a
    printed or electronic document obtained from the origin state's DMV portal confirming
    that (i) the vehicle is titled in the applicant's name, (ii) no unsatisfied lien is
    reflected in the origin ELT record, and (iii) the record has been flagged for transfer
    to Marion.

(c) The examiner verifies the ELT Release Confirmation is dated within 90 days of the
    application date, bears the applicant's name and the vehicle VIN, and shows lien
    status as RELEASED or NONE.

(d) Upon receipt of a complete ELT application, Marion requests an electronic title
    record transfer from the origin state DMV. Marion issues the Marion title upon
    confirmation of the transfer. Processing time is typically 5 business days but
    may vary.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

§ 2.1.4  ELT TRANSFERS WITH ACTIVE LIENS

If an ELT Release Confirmation presented by the applicant reflects an active or open
lien, the examiner shall not process the transfer and shall immediately refer to Admin.
Rule 5 (Exception Procedures) and Examiner Procedure Manual Chapter 5.

The applicant must obtain a new ELT Release Confirmation from the origin state, issued
after the lienholder has submitted an electronic lien release through the origin state's
lender system. The Marion DMV cannot release an ELT lien on behalf of a lienholder in
another state.

An examiner shall not accept the applicant's verbal assertion that a lien has been paid.
ELT lien status is determined solely by the ELT Release Confirmation document.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

§ 2.1.5  ODOMETER DISCLOSURE

Odometer disclosure is required for vehicles 10 model years old or newer, as counted from
the calendar year of application. Disclosure may appear on:
    — the face of the origin title (common for many jurisdictions);
    — Marion Form OD-1 (Odometer Disclosure Statement); or
    — within Marion Form TR-1, Block F, if the examiner enters the disclosed mileage there.

For vehicles older than 10 model years: the examiner marks Block F of Form TR-1 as
"EXEMPT — vehicle age exceeds odometer disclosure requirement."
