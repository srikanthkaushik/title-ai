WHEN TO USE FORM TR-1

Form TR-1 is required for ALL out-of-state title transfers processed at Marion DMV,
whether the transfer is a PURCHASE (vehicle bought from an out-of-state seller) or a
RELOCATION (owner moved to Marion with a vehicle titled in another state).

Do not use Form TR-1 for:
    — In-state title transfers (use Form IT-1)
    — Dealer title applications (use Form DT-1)
    — Duplicate title requests (use Form DUP-1)

COMPLETING FORM TR-1 — FIELD-BY-FIELD INSTRUCTIONS

BLOCK A — APPLICANT INFORMATION

A1. Full legal name (last, first, middle): Enter name exactly as it appears on the origin
    title or as buyer on the bill of sale.
A2. Marion address (street, city, zip): Must be a Marion address. P.O. Box not accepted
    as primary address.
A3. Date of birth: Required for identity verification.
A4. Marion driver's license number: Required. Out-of-state license is not accepted in
    this field — applicant must hold a Marion driver's license or present a Marion ID.
    (Note: If applicant has not yet obtained a Marion license, they must obtain one before
    completing a title transfer. Supervisor guidance may be needed.)

BLOCK B — VEHICLE INFORMATION

B1. Vehicle Identification Number (VIN): Copy from origin title or ELT Release Confirmation.
    17 characters. Must match Form TR-2 (VIN Inspection Certification) exactly.
B2. Model year: 4-digit year.
B3. Make.
B4. Model.
B5. Body type (sedan, SUV, pickup, etc.).
B6. Color (exterior).
B7. Current odometer reading: Applicant enters reading at time of application.

BLOCK C — TRANSFER TYPE

Check ONE box:
    ☐ PURCHASE — Vehicle was acquired by purchase from an out-of-state seller.
    ☐ RELOCATION — Owner moved to Marion; vehicle already owned and titled out of state.

If RELOCATION is checked: Block D (purchase information) must be LEFT BLANK.
If PURCHASE is checked: Block D must be completed.

BLOCK D — PURCHASE INFORMATION (PURCHASE ONLY; leave blank for RELOCATION)

D1. Seller's full legal name.
D2. Seller's address (street, city, state, zip).
D3. Date of sale: From bill of sale or purchase agreement.
D4. Purchase price: Declared dollar amount. Used for tax basis computation under
    Admin. Rule 7 § 7.2.1. If the declared price appears significantly below market
    value, the examiner will verify against NADA.

BLOCK E — ORIGIN TITLE INFORMATION

E1. Origin state.
E2. Origin title number (from paper title) OR ELT record number (from ELT Release
    Confirmation).
E3. Title issuance date (if shown on paper title).

BLOCK F — ODOMETER DISCLOSURE

Required for vehicles 10 model years old or newer.

F1. Odometer reading as of sale date or transfer date (must match bill of sale odometer
    if a purchase).
F2. Odometer accuracy certification: Applicant certifies the reading is accurate
    (or notes if the odometer has exceeded mechanical limits or is known to be incorrect).

For vehicles MORE THAN 10 model years old: Check the EXEMPT box. Enter the model year
and the current calendar year to confirm the age exemption.

BLOCK G — INSURANCE

G1. Insurance company name.
G2. Policy number.
G3. Expiration date of coverage.

The policy must cover the vehicle being transferred and must be in the applicant's name
(or show the applicant as a listed insured). Expired policies are not accepted.

BLOCK H — LIEN INFORMATION

Check ONE:
    ☐ NO LIEN — No lien recorded on origin title or ELT record.
    ☐ LIEN RELEASED — A lien existed but has been released. Attach lien release
      documentation (see Procedure Manual § 4.2).
    ☐ LIEN ACTIVE — [NOTE: If lien is active, do NOT complete Form TR-1 and submit.
      Stop processing and refer to Chapter 5 and Form TR-10.]

BLOCK I — APPLICANT SIGNATURE AND DATE

The applicant signs and dates here, certifying that all information provided is true
and correct. Unsigned TR-1 forms are not accepted.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

REQUIRED ATTACHMENTS

The following must accompany Form TR-1 at submission:
    1. Origin title (paper) or ELT Release Confirmation
    2. Form TR-2 (VIN Inspection Certification)
    3. Bill of sale or residency proof (depending on transfer type)
    4. Proof of Marion insurance
    5. Payment (fees + tax)
