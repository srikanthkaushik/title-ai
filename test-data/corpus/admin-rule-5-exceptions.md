§ 5.1  GENERAL

Certain conditions arising during an out-of-state title transfer application require
supervisor review before the applicant can be advised of the required documents, fee
total, or any other aspect of the transfer process. The purpose of this Rule is to ensure
that potential legal exposures — including undisclosed liens, incorrectly branded titles,
and documentation deficiencies — are identified and reviewed by a supervisor before the
agency commits to a course of action.

This Rule applies to all Marion DMV examiners processing out-of-state title transfers.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

§ 5.2  MANDATORY REFERRAL TRIGGERS

An examiner MUST refer the application to a supervisor, and MUST NOT proceed with
checklist generation or fee computation, when ANY of the following conditions is
present:

    (a) Active lien — paper title. The origin title displays a lien notation (stamp,
        printed lienholder name, or other indication of a recorded lien) and no
        corresponding lien release is present on the title face or as a separate
        document submitted with the application.

    (b) Branded title. Any brand designation appears on the origin title, regardless of
        whether that brand is listed in Marion's Brand Equivalency Schedule. This includes
        brands that Marion recognizes directly, brands that map to a Marion equivalent
        under the Equivalency Schedule, and brand terms that do not appear in the Schedule.
        The supervisor determines how to proceed with branded titles, including whether
        additional inspection is required.

    (c) Missing or unverifiable origin documentation. The applicant cannot produce the
        origin title (claiming loss, theft, or delivery failure), the ELT Release
        Confirmation is unavailable or cannot be verified, or the examiner has reasonable
        doubt about the authenticity of the presented documents.

    (d) Unrecognized origin state. The vehicle's origin state does not appear in the
        current Marion DMV Recognized States list (Admin. Rule 4). Examiners shall not
        make independent determinations of whether an unlisted state is acceptable.

    (e) VIN discrepancy. The VIN as recorded on the completed Form TR-2 (VIN Inspection
        Certification) does not match the VIN on the origin title or ELT record.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

§ 5.3  EXAMINER CONDUCT DURING REFERRAL

5.3.1  Upon identifying any condition in § 5.2, the examiner shall:
    (1) Suspend processing of the application immediately.
    (2) Complete Form TR-10 (Supervisor Exception Referral), Blocks 1 through 4, as
        specified in § 5.4 below.
    (3) Notify the applicant that the application requires additional review, using the
        following or equivalent language: "We need to review some details with a supervisor.
        We will contact you within two business days." The examiner shall NOT disclose
        the specific nature of the exception to the applicant.

5.3.2  An examiner SHALL NOT:
    — Tell the applicant what documents are required or what fees are owed, prior to
      supervisor clearance.
    — Advise the applicant on how to resolve the exception (e.g., how to obtain a lien
      release), as such advice could be incorrect before the supervisor has assessed the
      situation.
    — Process any portion of the application while a referral is pending.

5.3.3  Conditional checklist permitted. Notwithstanding § 5.3.2, an examiner MAY provide
the applicant with a list of documents that will likely be required once the exception is
resolved. Such a list MUST be clearly and prominently marked:

    "CONDITIONAL — SUBJECT TO SUPERVISOR REVIEW.
     DO NOT ACT ON THIS LIST UNTIL THE SUPERVISOR HAS CLEARED YOUR TRANSACTION.
     This list is provided for planning purposes only and is not a final determination
     of requirements."

The examiner should explain verbally that the list is provisional and that the supervisor's
review may change what is required.

§ 5.4  COMPLETING FORM TR-10 (SUPERVISOR EXCEPTION REFERRAL)

The examiner completes Blocks 1 through 4 before routing to the supervisor.

    Block 1: Applicant information — name, phone number, date of visit, examiner ID number.

    Block 2: Vehicle information — VIN, year, make, model, origin state, origin title number
             (or ELT record number if applicable).

    Block 3: Exception type — check all boxes that apply from the following list:
             ☐ Active lien — paper title
             ☐ Active lien — ELT record
             ☐ Branded title — recognized (brand term matches Marion nomenclature)
             ☐ Branded title — equivalent (origin term differs from Marion term)
             ☐ Branded title — UNRECOGNIZED (not in Marion's Equivalency Schedule)
             ☐ VIN discrepancy (mismatch between TR-2 and origin title)
             ☐ Missing or unverifiable origin documentation
             ☐ Unrecognized origin state
             ☐ Other (describe in Block 4)

    Block 4: What the examiner has confirmed to date. Enter all information verified before
             the exception was identified: title form (paper/ELT), lien holder name if
             visible, brand name as it appears on the title, ELT lien status if shown on
             Release Confirmation. Leave nothing blank that the examiner can determine.

    Block 5: Supervisor disposition — LEFT BLANK by examiner. Supervisor completes upon
             review (see § 5.6).

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

§ 5.5  TURNAROUND REQUIREMENT

The supervisor must review the TR-10 and respond to the examiner within two (2) business
days of receipt. If the supervisor does not respond within this period, the examiner shall
follow up directly with the supervisor's office.

The applicant may be asked to return to the DMV office once the supervisor has reached a
disposition. The examiner may call or email the applicant using the contact information
in Block 1.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

§ 5.6  SUPERVISOR DISPOSITION CODES

The supervisor enters one of the following codes in Block 5 of Form TR-10:

    CLEARED — The exception has been reviewed and resolved. The examiner may proceed
              with the standard transfer process. The supervisor notes in Block 5 how
              the exception was resolved (e.g., "lien confirmed released per lienholder
              call," "brand confirmed as Marion 'Reconstructed'").

    PEND-DOCS — Additional documentation is required from the applicant before the
                supervisor can clear the exception. The supervisor lists the required
                documents in Block 5. Examiner contacts applicant.

    DENIED — The transfer cannot proceed at this time. The supervisor records the reason
             in Block 5. Examiner communicates denial to applicant using neutral language;
             applicant may appeal per Marion DMV appeals procedure (Admin. Rule 12).
