§ 2.4.1  APPLICABILITY

Emissions inspection requirements apply to all motor vehicles registering in Marion,
including vehicles transferred from out-of-state titles. The registration county and
vehicle model year determine whether an emissions test is required.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

§ 2.4.2  COUNTY CLASSIFICATION

Marion counties are classified as follows for purposes of this Rule:

METRO COUNTIES (emissions testing required for qualifying vehicles):
    • Marion County
    • Riverside County
    • Capital County

RURAL COUNTIES (emissions testing NOT required — all vehicles exempt):
    All Marion counties not listed above.

The county applicable to an out-of-state transfer is the county of the applicant's
Marion address (for relocation transfers) or the intended county of vehicle registration
(for purchase transfers).

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

§ 2.4.3  VEHICLE AGE EXEMPTION

Effective January 1, 2023: A motor vehicle with a model year that is 25 or more years
prior to the calendar year of registration is EXEMPT from emissions testing, regardless
of the county of registration.

Calculation: Vehicle is exempt if (Calendar Year of Registration) − (Vehicle Model Year) ≥ 25.

Example (for calendar year 2026): Vehicles with model year 2001 or earlier are exempt.
A 1998 model year vehicle applying for registration in 2026 has an age of 28 years and
is therefore exempt from emissions testing under this section.
A 2005 model year vehicle applying for registration in 2026 has an age of 21 years and
is NOT exempt; emissions testing is required if the vehicle is registering in a metro county.

NOTE: This 25-year threshold replaces the prior 20-year threshold that was in effect
under the superseded version of this Rule (July 1, 2018 – December 31, 2022).

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

§ 2.4.4  ADDITIONAL EXEMPTIONS

The following vehicle types are exempt from emissions testing regardless of county or
model year:
    (a) New vehicles — current model year or the immediately prior model year;
    (b) Battery electric vehicles (BEV) and hydrogen fuel cell vehicles;
    (c) Motorcycles and mopeds with engine displacement under 50cc;
    (d) Farm vehicles used exclusively for agricultural purposes and not licensed for
        highway use.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

§ 2.4.5  PROOF OF COMPLIANCE

An applicant whose vehicle is subject to emissions testing under this Rule must present
a valid Marion Emissions Certificate (Form EMIT-1) issued by a Marion-authorized emissions
testing station. The certificate must:
    — be dated within 90 days of the title/registration application date;
    — bear the vehicle VIN matching the origin title;
    — show a passing result.

A list of authorized stations is available from Marion DMV offices and at
www.mariondmv.gov/emissions-stations. See also Admin. Rule 3 for station certification
requirements.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

§ 2.4.6  ORIGIN-STATE EMISSIONS PROGRAMS — NOT ACCEPTED

An emissions test performed in the origin state — regardless of that state's emissions
requirements or the recency of the test — does NOT satisfy Marion's emissions testing
requirement.

Marion requires a Marion-administered or Marion-authorized emissions test. This applies
even where the origin state has its own emissions program, and it applies even where
the origin state does NOT have an emissions program. The existence or nonexistence of an
emissions testing requirement in the vehicle's state of origin is irrelevant to the
applicability of Marion's requirement.

Examples:
— A vehicle from Halloway (which does not operate a state emissions program) is still
  subject to Marion's emissions requirement if it is registering in a Marion metro county
  and meets the model year threshold.
— A vehicle from Verdana (which does operate a state emissions program) must obtain a
  Marion test; the Verdana test certificate is not accepted.
