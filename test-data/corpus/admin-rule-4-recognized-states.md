§ 4.1  PURPOSE AND SCOPE

Marion accepts out-of-state title transfers from jurisdictions whose title systems,
lien records, and documentation standards meet the requirements established by the
Marion DMV Administrator. This Rule establishes the current list of recognized states.

Any state or jurisdiction NOT listed in § 4.2 of this Rule is an unrecognized jurisdiction
for purposes of out-of-state title transfers. Vehicles from unrecognized jurisdictions
require supervisor referral before processing, pursuant to Admin. Rule 5.2(d). Examiners
may not process a transfer from an unrecognized jurisdiction without supervisor clearance.

§ 4.3  EFFECTIVE DATE AND UPDATES

This list is current as of January 1, 2025. The Administrator reviews and updates this
list annually. Additions or removals are published as amendments to this Rule.

Examiners presenting applications from states not in § 4.2 — including states that may
have been recognized under prior versions of this Rule — must refer to a supervisor.
Do not rely on memory or prior versions of Admin. Rule 4 to determine recognition status.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

§ 4.4  ELT STATES — SPECIAL NOTE

States designated "ELT" or "Mixed" in the Title Form column may not issue paper titles
for vehicles in their ELT program. Examiners shall not require a paper title from an
applicant whose vehicle was titled in one of these states if the vehicle is in the ELT
program. Accept the ELT Release Confirmation in lieu of a paper title, consistent with
Admin. Rule 2.1 § 2.1.3.
