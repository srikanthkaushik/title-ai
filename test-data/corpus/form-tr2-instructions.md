WHEN TO USE FORM TR-2

Form TR-2 is required for every out-of-state title transfer. No exception is made for
vehicle age, purchase price, brand status, or transfer type. A completed, valid Form TR-2
must accompany every Form TR-1 submission. Applications submitted without a valid TR-2
will be rejected as incomplete.

The customer obtains Form TR-2 from a Marion DMV office (or download from mariondmv.gov),
brings it to an authorized inspector, and submits the completed form with the TR-1
application package.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

WHO MAY COMPLETE FORM TR-2 (AUTHORIZED INSPECTORS ONLY)

Form TR-2 may only be completed by one of the following:

(a) A Marion-licensed Motor Vehicle Inspector
    License number prefix: "MVI-"
    Must hold a current, valid license on the date of inspection.

(b) An active, sworn Marion law enforcement officer
    (Municipal, county, or state level)
    Badge number required in Block A.

(c) A Marion-licensed motor vehicle dealer
    Dealer license number prefix: "DLR-"

Form TR-2 completed by anyone other than the persons listed above — including mechanics,
body shops, origin-state inspectors, private citizens, or the customer — is NOT valid.
The examiner will reject an invalid TR-2 and the application will be incomplete.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

VIN LOCATIONS INSPECTED

The inspector physically examines the following locations on the vehicle:

Primary (required):
    Dashboard VIN plate — metal or plastic plate affixed to the driver's side of the
    dashboard, visible from outside through the windshield.

Secondary (required):
    Driver-side door jamb sticker — manufacturer's VIN label on the door jamb or frame.

Tertiary (if accessible without tools):
    Engine block — VIN stamped or labeled on engine block. If inaccessible without
    removing components, the inspector marks "N/A — inaccessible" in Block B.

All readable VIN locations must match each other and match the VIN on the origin title
or ELT Release Confirmation, character for character.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

COMPLETING FORM TR-2 — FIELD INSTRUCTIONS

BLOCK A — INSPECTOR INFORMATION

A1. Inspector's printed full name.
A2. License/badge/dealer number:
    — MVI-XXXXX (licensed motor vehicle inspector)
    — Badge number (law enforcement officer — also enter agency name)
    — DLR-XXXXX (licensed dealer)
A3. Date of inspection. The TR-2 is valid for 90 days from this date.

BLOCK B — VIN READINGS

Record the VIN as physically observed at each location:

B1. Dashboard plate VIN: [enter 17-character VIN]
B2. Door jamb sticker VIN: [enter 17-character VIN]
B3. Engine block VIN: [enter VIN, or mark "N/A — inaccessible"]

If all three (or two readable) locations match the origin title VIN: proceed to Block C.
If any location does NOT match the title VIN: check the DISCREPANCY box and complete
Block D. Do not certify in Block C if a discrepancy exists.

BLOCK C — INSPECTOR CERTIFICATION

The inspector signs and dates, certifying:
    "I have physically inspected the VIN(s) noted above and they match the VIN on the
    origin title / ELT Release Confirmation. The vehicle description (year, make, model)
    is consistent with the title."

Do not sign Block C if a discrepancy is noted in Block B.

BLOCK D — DISCREPANCY NOTES (complete only if discrepancy found)

Describe the discrepancy: which location does not match, what was observed vs. what
the title shows. The examiner will refer the application to a supervisor upon receipt
of a TR-2 with Block D completed.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

VALIDITY AND FEE

Valid for: 90 days from the Block A inspection date.
Expired TR-2 (more than 90 days old): application is incomplete; new inspection required.

Fee: $15.00, collected by Marion DMV at the time of the TR-1 application.
The inspector does NOT charge a DMV fee; any inspector fee is a separate arrangement
between the customer and inspector.
