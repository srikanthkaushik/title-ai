1.1  PURPOSE

This manual establishes the procedures Marion DMV examiners follow when processing
out-of-state motor vehicle title transfers. It translates the Marion Vehicle Code
(§§ 301–330) and the Administrative Rules (AR-2.1, AR-2.4, AR-4, AR-5, AR-7, AR-9)
into step-by-step examiner actions. Where this manual conflicts with an Administrative
Rule, the Rule governs.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

1.2  SCENARIO CLASSIFICATION — FIRST STEP AT THE COUNTER

Before examining any documents, the examiner must determine which transfer scenario
applies. Ask the customer:

    "Did you purchase this vehicle from a seller in another state,
     or are you bringing it with you after moving to Marion?"

Two scenarios:

PURCHASE: The vehicle was sold to the customer by an out-of-state seller. A bill of
sale or purchase agreement exists. Tax basis is the purchase price (or NADA if declared
price is below threshold — see Admin. Rule 7 § 7.2.1).

RELOCATION: The customer owned the vehicle before moving to Marion. No purchase occurred.
Tax basis is NADA Clean Retail value (see Admin. Rule 7 § 7.2.2).

If the customer's answer is unclear or involves a gift, inheritance, or other non-purchase
transfer, document the customer's explanation verbatim and refer to supervisor before
proceeding. Do not classify ambiguous transfers independently.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

1.3  INITIAL DOCUMENT REVIEW

Once the scenario is classified, the examiner conducts a rapid initial review:

Step 1 — Title form: Is the customer presenting a paper title or an ELT Release
    Confirmation? (See Admin. Rule 4 for which states use ELT.)

Step 2 — Lien status: Does the title face (or ELT Release Confirmation) show any lien
    notation? Is a release present? (See Admin. Rule 2.1 § 2.1.2 for paper; § 2.1.3 for ELT.)

Step 3 — Brand status: Does the title face show any brand designation? Any stamp,
    notation, or printed brand field? (See Admin. Rule 5.2(b) — ANY brand triggers referral.)

Step 4 — VIN: Does the customer have a completed Form TR-2 (VIN Inspection Certification)?
    Is the VIN on TR-2 legible? (Full VIN check happens at document assembly, not here.)

Stop at any step if a triggering condition (lien, brand) is found. Refer to Chapter 5 and
Admin. Rule 5 before proceeding.
