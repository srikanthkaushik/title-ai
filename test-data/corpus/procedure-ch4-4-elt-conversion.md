§ 4.4 — ELT CONVERSION (ELT-ORIGIN TITLE, PURCHASE OR RELOCATION)

SCENARIO:   Transfer Type: PURCHASE or RELOCATION
            Origin Title Form: ELT (Electronic Lien and Title)
            Lien Status: NONE or RELEASED IN ELT SYSTEM
            Brand: CLEAN (any brand triggers supervisor referral — see Chapter 5)

This section applies when the vehicle's origin state participates in an electronic title
system and has never issued a paper title for the vehicle. Verdana is an ELT state.
See Admin. Rule 4 for the complete list of ELT and mixed-system states.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

4.4.1  WHAT ELT MEANS — EXAMINER BACKGROUND

In ELT states, the vehicle title is maintained as an electronic record in the origin
state's central system. No paper title is printed and mailed to the owner.

This means:
    • The customer WILL NOT have a paper title. Do not ask for one, and do not reject
      the application because no paper title is presented.
    • The title record exists in the origin state's database, not as a physical document.
    • Liens are recorded and released electronically in the same system.
    • A lienholder holds the "title" in the sense that the ELT system reflects their lien,
      but there is no physical document to hold.

Marion recognizes ELT records from states listed in Admin. Rule 4 (Recognized States).
Only accept ELT Release Confirmations from recognized states.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

4.4.2  WHAT THE CUSTOMER BRINGS IN PLACE OF A PAPER TITLE

The customer presents an ELT Release Confirmation — a document printed or emailed from
the origin state's DMV portal confirming:
    (a) the vehicle is titled in the customer's name in the origin state's ELT system;
    (b) any lien has been released (status shows RELEASED or NONE);
    (c) the title record has been flagged as available for transfer to another state
        (in some states this is called a "transfer release" or "out-of-state release").

The ELT Release Confirmation must:
    • Be dated within 90 days of the Marion application date.
    • Show the customer's name and the vehicle VIN matching the application.
    • Show lien status as RELEASED or NONE (not ACTIVE or OPEN).

Marion DMV cannot accept the customer's verbal assurance that a lien has been released
or that the vehicle is in their name. The ELT Release Confirmation is required.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

4.4.3  REQUIRED DOCUMENTS

☐  1. ELT RELEASE CONFIRMATION from the origin state (in place of paper title).
       • Dated within 90 days of application.
       • Customer name and VIN shown.
       • Lien status: RELEASED or NONE. (If ACTIVE: STOP — refer to Chapter 5.)

☐  2. BILL OF SALE or PURCHASE AGREEMENT (PURCHASE transfers only).
       • Same requirements as § 4.1.1 Item 2.
       (RELOCATION transfers: skip this item; proceed to residency proof below.)

☐  2A. PROOF OF MARION RESIDENCY (RELOCATION transfers only, in lieu of bill of sale).
        • Same requirements as § 4.3.1 Item 2 (two forms of residency proof).

☐  3. MARION FORM TR-1 — Out-of-State Title Transfer Application.
       • Block C: PURCHASE or RELOCATION as applicable.
       • Block D: Purchase information (PURCHASE only); leave blank for RELOCATION.
       • Block E: Origin state and ELT record number (from the ELT Release Confirmation).
       • Block F: Odometer disclosure or EXEMPT as applicable.
       • Block H: Lien = NONE or RELEASED (with ELT Release Confirmation attached).

☐  4. MARION FORM TR-2 — VIN Inspection Certification.
       Required even for ELT transfers — VIN must be physically verified regardless of
       title form. Dated within 90 days of application.

☐  5. PROOF OF MARION INSURANCE — as specified in § 4.1.1 Item 5.

☐  6. PAYMENT — same fee structure as § 4.1.1 Item 7.
       (Note: no lien release processing fee for ELT lien releases — lien released
       electronically in origin state; Marion does not process it.)

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

4.4.4  MARION'S PROCESSING STEPS (INTERNAL — NOT CUSTOMER-FACING)

After the examiner accepts a complete ELT application, Marion DMV:
    (1) Submits an electronic title transfer request to the origin state's DMV system.
    (2) The origin state's system cancels or archives the ELT record.
    (3) Marion issues a Marion paper title (or Marion ELT record, per customer election)
        after confirmation from the origin state.

Processing time is typically 5 business days from application acceptance, though delays
can occur if the origin state's system is slow to confirm. The examiner should inform
the customer of the typical timeline but not guarantee a specific date.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

4.4.5  ELT TRANSFER WITH ACTIVE LIEN — DO NOT USE THIS SECTION

If the ELT Release Confirmation shows an ACTIVE or OPEN lien, this section (§ 4.4)
does not apply. Stop immediately. Refer to Chapter 5 and Admin. Rule 5.2(a).

The lienholder must submit an electronic lien release through the origin state's lender
system. Once released, the customer obtains a new ELT Release Confirmation showing
the lien as RELEASED. Then the examiner may proceed using § 4.4.
