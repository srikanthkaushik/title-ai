Sec. 330. VEHICLE IDENTIFICATION NUMBER VERIFICATION; OUT-OF-STATE TRANSFERS.

(a) Mandatory inspection. A physical inspection of the vehicle identification number (VIN)
shall be required for all motor vehicles presented for out-of-state title transfer under
§§ 301–305 of this Code. No exception is provided for vehicle age, brand status, purchase
price, or any other factor. The VIN inspection requirement applies universally to every
out-of-state title transfer processed in Marion.

(b) Authorized inspectors. The VIN inspection required by this section shall be performed
only by:
    (1) a Marion-licensed motor vehicle inspector holding a current license issued by the
        Department under Chapter 5 of this Code;
    (2) an active, sworn law enforcement officer of any Marion municipal, county, or state
        law enforcement agency; or
    (3) a motor vehicle dealer licensed by Marion under Chapter 6 of this Code.

Inspections performed by officials of the origin jurisdiction, by private garages not
holding a Marion inspector license, or by any other person not listed in subsection (b) are
not accepted for purposes of this section.

(c) Required documentation. The results of the VIN inspection shall be recorded on Marion
Department of Motor Vehicles Form TR-2 (VIN Inspection Certification). A completed and
signed Form TR-2 must accompany the applicant's Form TR-1 (Out-of-State Title Transfer
Application). Applications received without a valid Form TR-2 shall be rejected as
incomplete.

(d) VIN match required. The VIN as physically observed on the vehicle by the authorized
inspector must match exactly the VIN appearing on the origin title or electronic title
record. For purposes of this section, the VIN shall be verified at a minimum at:
    (1) the dashboard VIN plate visible through the windshield on the driver's side;
    (2) the VIN label affixed to the driver-side door jamb.

Where the inspector observes a discrepancy between the VIN on the vehicle and the VIN on
the origin title, the examiner shall not process the transfer and shall refer the matter to
a supervisor pursuant to Admin. Rule 5.2(e). The applicant shall not be informed of the
specific nature of the discrepancy pending supervisor review.
