MARION DMV EXAMINER PROCEDURE MANUAL
CHAPTER 4 — DOCUMENT CHECKLISTS
§ 4.1 — PURCHASE TRANSFER / PAPER TITLE / NO LIEN / CLEAN TITLE

Document ID: PM-CH4-1 | Authority: Admin. Rules 2.1, 2.4, 7, 9 | Revision: 2024-03

                    ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
                    Examiner Procedure Manual | Chapter 4 — Checklists | §4.1 Purchase/Paper/No Lien | Rev. 2024-03
                    ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

SCENARIO:   Transfer Type: PURCHASE
            Origin Title Form: PAPER
            Lien Status: NONE (no lien notation on title face)
            Brand: CLEAN (no brand designation)

Use this checklist when the customer purchased the vehicle from an out-of-state seller,
holds a physical paper title with no lien notation, and the title bears no brand. This
is the base case for paper-title purchase transfers.

If ANY lien is present (even if reportedly released), use § 4.2.
If an ELT Release Confirmation is presented instead of a paper title, use § 4.4.
If a brand appears on the title, STOP and refer to Chapter 5 before proceeding.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

4.1.1  REQUIRED DOCUMENTS — Customer must produce ALL of the following:

☐  1. ORIGIN TITLE — Original paper title issued by the origin state, properly assigned
       to the buyer. Requirements:
       • All prior assignments completed in ink (no blank assignments in the chain).
       • Title face is in the customer's name, or the last blank assignment is
         completed with the customer as buyer.
       • Odometer disclosure present (required if vehicle is 10 model years old or newer;
         older vehicles are exempt — confirm by model year).
       • No lien notation visible anywhere on the title face.

☐  2. BILL OF SALE or PURCHASE AGREEMENT showing:
       • Year, make, model, and VIN of the vehicle.
       • Purchase price (used for tax basis per Admin. Rule 7 § 7.2.1).
       • Full name and address of seller.
       • Full name and address of buyer (must match applicant).
       • Date of sale.
       Handwritten bills of sale are acceptable if legible and complete. Dealer purchase
       agreements are acceptable.

☐  3. MARION FORM TR-1 — Out-of-State Title Transfer Application, completed by customer.
       • Block C: Transfer type = PURCHASE.
       • Block D: Purchase information completed (seller name, address, purchase price, date).
       • Block F: Odometer disclosure completed, or EXEMPT box checked for older vehicles.
       • Block H: Lien information = NONE (or no active lien box checked).
       • Customer signature and date in Block I.

☐  4. MARION FORM TR-2 — VIN Inspection Certification, completed by authorized inspector.
       • Must be dated within 90 days of today's application date.
       • VINs in Block B must match the origin title VIN exactly.
       • No discrepancy noted in Block D.

☐  5. PROOF OF MARION INSURANCE — Policy declaration page or insurance identification card:
       • In the customer's name (or showing customer as listed insured).
       • For the subject vehicle (VIN must match or insurer confirms).
       • Coverage meets Marion minimum requirements (not expired).

☐  6. ODOMETER DISCLOSURE — Required for vehicles 10 model years old or newer.
       May appear on: the origin title face; a separate Marion Form OD-1; or Block F
       of Form TR-1. For vehicles older than 10 model years: confirm EXEMPT box marked.

☐  7. PAYMENT — Due at time of application:
       • Title transfer fee: $25.00 (Form TR-1)
       • VIN inspection fee: $15.00 (Form TR-2)
       • Base registration fee: $45.00 (vehicles ≤ 5,000 lbs) or $65.00 (5,001–8,500 lbs)
       • Sales tax: computed per Admin. Rule 7 (5.5% of taxable value, less reciprocity
         credit if origin state has agreement — see Tax Reciprocity Schedule)
       • If emissions test required (see note below): $35.00 to the testing station
         (not to DMV; customer pays station directly and presents EMIT-1 certificate)

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

4.1.2  ADDITIONAL REQUIREMENTS — READ BEFORE ISSUING CHECKLIST

EMISSIONS TESTING (conditional):
    Required IF: Registration county is Marion County, Riverside County, or Capital County
    AND vehicle model year is less than 25 years old (i.e., model year is 2002 or later
    for a 2026 transfer).

    If emissions required: Add to checklist —
    ☐  8. MARION EMISSIONS CERTIFICATE (Form EMIT-1) issued by a Marion-authorized
           testing station. Dated within 90 days of application. Passing result required.

    If NOT required (rural county or vehicle ≥ 25 years old): No emissions item needed.
    Note this on the checklist for the customer's reference.

LIEN CONFIRMATION:
    Before issuing this checklist, confirm that the title face shows no lien stamp,
    lienholder name, or other indication of a recorded lien. Even a faded or partially
    legible lien stamp should be referred to Chapter 5. Do not assume a lien is released
    without a lien release document.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

4.1.3  EXAMINER NOTES

• The bill of sale and origin title together are the foundation of this transaction. If
  either is missing or incomplete, the application cannot proceed without supervisor review.

• Do not accept a photocopy of the origin title. The original must be surrendered to Marion
  DMV upon issuance of the Marion title.

• If the title is from an ELT state (Verdana, or another state listed in Admin. Rule 4
  as ELT), the customer will NOT have a paper title. Redirect to § 4.4.
