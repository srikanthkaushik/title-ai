NOTES

1. Sales and use tax is NOT listed in the table above. Tax is computed separately under
   Admin. Rule 7 and the Tax Reciprocity Schedule, and is owed in addition to all fees
   listed here. Tax is collected by Marion DMV at the time of application.

2. Registration fees listed are base rates for passenger vehicles. Additional fees may
   apply for specialty plates, personalized plates, or county add-on assessments. These
   are not covered in this Rule.

3. The emissions test fee ($35.00) is paid to the testing station, not to Marion DMV.
   The DMV does not collect or refund emissions fees.

4. This schedule applies to out-of-state transfers only. Fees for in-state title transfers,
   dealer transactions, and other transactions are governed by separate schedules.
