WHEN TO USE FORM TR-10

Form TR-10 must be completed whenever an examiner identifies an exception condition
during an out-of-state title transfer application. Exception conditions triggering TR-10:

    ☐ Active lien — paper title (lien notation with no release present)
    ☐ Active lien — ELT record (ELT Release Confirmation shows ACTIVE/OPEN lien)
    ☐ Branded title — recognized (brand term matches Marion nomenclature)
    ☐ Branded title — equivalent (origin term differs from Marion term)
    ☐ Branded title — UNRECOGNIZED (not in Marion's Brand Equivalency Schedule)
    ☐ VIN discrepancy (TR-2 VIN does not match origin title VIN)
    ☐ Missing or unverifiable origin documentation
    ☐ Unrecognized origin state (state not in Admin. Rule 4 Recognized States list)
    ☐ Other (describe in Block 4)

The examiner completes Blocks 1 through 4 before routing to the supervisor.
Block 5 is completed by the supervisor and returned to the examiner.
No fee is charged to the applicant for supervisor review.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

COMPLETING FORM TR-10 — BLOCK-BY-BLOCK INSTRUCTIONS

BLOCK 1 — APPLICANT AND VISIT INFORMATION

1a. Applicant's full name (as on Form TR-1 Block A1).
1b. Applicant's phone number (for supervisor or examiner to contact applicant).
1c. Applicant's email address (optional; for written communication of supervisor decision).
1d. Date of DMV visit.
1e. Examiner ID number.
1f. DMV office location.

BLOCK 2 — VEHICLE INFORMATION

2a. Vehicle VIN (copy from origin title or ELT Release Confirmation).
2b. Model year, make, model.
2c. Origin state.
2d. Origin title number or ELT record number.
2e. Transfer type: check PURCHASE or RELOCATION.

BLOCK 3 — EXCEPTION TYPE (check ALL that apply)

    ☐ Active lien — paper title
    ☐ Active lien — ELT record
    ☐ Branded title — recognized (origin brand = Marion brand)
    ☐ Branded title — equivalent (origin brand differs from Marion term; equivalency
          schedule applies — record origin term and Marion equivalent in Block 4)
    ☐ Branded title — UNRECOGNIZED (brand not in equivalency schedule)
    ☐ VIN discrepancy (describe in Block 4)
    ☐ Missing or unverifiable origin documentation
    ☐ Unrecognized origin state
    ☐ Other

NOTE: Multiple boxes may be checked if more than one exception condition is present.
For example, a vehicle with both an active lien AND a brand would have two boxes checked.

BLOCK 4 — WHAT EXAMINER HAS CONFIRMED

Enter all information the examiner has verified before identifying the exception. Be
specific. Examples:

    — Active lien scenario: "Title is paper Crestwood title. Owner name matches customer.
      Lien notation reads: MIDWEST AUTO FINANCE, lien date 2022-04-01. No release
      endorsement on title face. No separate lien release presented."

    — Branded title: "Halloway paper title. Brand field reads: 'Rebuilt'. Per Brand
      Equivalency Guide, Halloway 'Rebuilt' = Marion 'Reconstructed.' Supervisor review
      required per Admin. Rule 5.2(b)."

    — ELT active lien: "Verdana ELT Release Confirmation presented. Lien status shows:
      ACTIVE — First National Lending, lien date 2021-09-15. Lien not released."

    — Unrecognized state: "Origin title purports to be from [state name]. State not
      found in Admin. Rule 4 recognized states list."

Leave nothing blank that the examiner can determine. The more information in Block 4,
the faster the supervisor can reach a disposition.

BLOCK 5 — SUPERVISOR DISPOSITION (SUPERVISOR COMPLETES; leave blank)

The supervisor records one of the following disposition codes and supporting notes:

    CLEARED — Exception resolved; examiner may proceed with the transfer.
              Supervisor notes how the exception was resolved.

    PEND-DOCS — Additional documentation required. Supervisor lists what is needed
                from the applicant. Examiner contacts applicant.

    DENIED — Transfer cannot proceed. Supervisor records reason. Examiner notifies
             applicant using neutral language per Procedure Manual Chapter 5.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

CONDITIONAL CHECKLIST — OPTIONAL ATTACHMENT TO TR-10

The examiner MAY attach a conditional checklist to Form TR-10 for the applicant's
planning purposes. The checklist lists documents that will likely be required once the
exception is resolved. It MUST be clearly marked with the following language on the
top of the page, in bold or highlighted text:

    "CONDITIONAL — SUBJECT TO SUPERVISOR REVIEW.
     DO NOT ACT ON THIS LIST UNTIL THE SUPERVISOR HAS CLEARED YOUR TRANSACTION.
     This list is provided for planning purposes only and is not a final determination
     of the documents required. The supervisor's review may result in changes."

The examiner should explain verbally to the applicant that the list is provisional.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

CUSTOMER COMMUNICATION SCRIPT

Use the following or equivalent language when informing an applicant of the referral:
    "We need to review some details with a supervisor. We will contact you within
    two business days at the number you've provided."

Do NOT say:
    — "Your title has a lien on it."
    — "Your title is branded."
    — "Your state isn't in our system."
    — Any statement that reveals the specific exception type.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

TURNAROUND

The supervisor must complete Block 5 and return Form TR-10 to the examiner within
two (2) business days of receipt. If no response is received, the examiner follows up
directly with the supervisor's office. The applicant may be contacted once the
supervisor's disposition is received.
