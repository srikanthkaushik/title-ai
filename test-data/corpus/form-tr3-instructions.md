WHEN TO USE FORM TR-3

Form TR-3 is used when a lienholder releases a vehicle lien through Marion DMV's registered
lender system. It is ONE of three acceptable methods of establishing lien release for
out-of-state paper title transfers. (See Procedure Manual § 4.2 for all three methods.)

Form TR-3 is NOT required when:
    — The lien is released by endorsement on the origin title face (most common method).
    — The lien is released by a separate lienholder letter on letterhead.
    — The lien is released electronically in an ELT system (ELT Release Confirmation
      is the document for that scenario).

Form TR-3 IS used when:
    — The lienholder is registered with Marion DMV's registered lender system AND
    — The lienholder is releasing the lien through that system.

IMPORTANT: Form TR-3 is NOT a substitute for an origin-state lien release. If the origin
title shows an unreleased lien and the lienholder has NOT registered with Marion DMV's
lender system, Form TR-3 cannot be used. The examiner must refer to Chapter 5.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

WHO COMPLETES FORM TR-3

Form TR-3 is completed by the lienholder (or by Marion DMV on behalf of a registered
lienholder if the lienholder has authorized online submission). The customer does NOT
complete Form TR-3.

Registered lenders: Financial institutions and other lienholders may register with Marion
DMV's lender system to enable electronic lien release. Contact Marion DMV's Lender
Registration Office for registration information. Not all lenders are registered;
unregistered lienholders release liens by title endorsement or separate letter.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

COMPLETING FORM TR-3 — FIELD INSTRUCTIONS

BLOCK A — LIENHOLDER INFORMATION

A1. Lienholder's full legal name (must match the name shown on the origin title).
A2. Marion DMV lender registration number.
A3. Lienholder's mailing address.
A4. Contact name and phone number (for examiner follow-up if needed).

BLOCK B — VEHICLE INFORMATION

B1. Vehicle VIN (17 characters).
B2. Year, make, model.
B3. Origin title number.

BLOCK C — LIEN INFORMATION

C1. Original lien date (date lien was recorded).
C2. Original lien amount.
C3. Lien account number (lienholder's internal reference).

BLOCK D — RELEASE CERTIFICATION

D1. Lienholder certification: "The lien referenced above has been paid in full and is
    hereby released. [Lienholder name] authorizes Marion DMV to process the title
    transfer free of this lien."
D2. Authorized officer's signature and printed name.
D3. Officer's title (e.g., "Vice President — Auto Lending").
D4. Date of release certification.

BLOCK E — MARION DMV ACKNOWLEDGMENT

Completed by the Marion DMV examiner or processing unit upon receipt of the release.
Stamped with the Marion DMV official seal and dated. The customer does not complete
Block E.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

FEE

$5.00 lien release processing fee is charged to the applicant when Form TR-3 is used.
No processing fee is charged when the lien is released by title endorsement or separate
lienholder letter (those methods do not use the Marion DMV lender system).
