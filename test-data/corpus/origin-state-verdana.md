OVERVIEW

Verdana eliminated paper titles for passenger motor vehicles effective January 1, 2015.
All passenger vehicle titles in Verdana are maintained in the Verdana Electronic Lien
and Title System (VerdanaELT). Customers from Verdana will not have a paper title.
This is expected and correct — do not ask for one and do not reject the application
because no paper title is presented.

Verdana is listed in Admin. Rule 4 as an ELT state. See Admin. Rule 2.1 § 2.1.3 and
Examiner Procedure Manual § 4.4 for ELT transfer procedures.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

TITLE SYSTEM: ELT (ELECTRONIC LIEN AND TITLE)

How Verdana titles work:
• The title record is held in VerdanaELT, maintained by the Verdana Department of
  Motor Vehicles.
• The owner's name, vehicle VIN, and lien status (if any) are recorded electronically.
• No paper document exists for the customer to produce; the title IS the electronic record.

What the customer brings to Marion DMV:
• An ELT Release Confirmation printed or emailed from the Verdana DMV portal.
  (Verdana portal: portal.verdana.gov/elt-release — for customer reference;
   Marion DMV examiners do not access this portal directly.)
• The ELT Release Confirmation confirms: (a) vehicle is in customer's name; (b) lien
  status; (c) record is available for out-of-state transfer.
• The ELT Release Confirmation must be dated within 90 days of the Marion application.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

LIEN PROCEDURES IN VERDANA

Active liens in Verdana are recorded in VerdanaELT. When a lienholder is satisfied,
they submit an electronic release through Verdana's secure lender portal
(lenders.verdana.gov/elt-release — for lender use; not accessible to customers or
Marion DMV examiners).

Electronic lien release in Verdana typically processes within 3 business days of the
lender's submission. After the lien is released in VerdanaELT, the customer may
obtain a new ELT Release Confirmation showing lien status as RELEASED or NONE.

If a customer presents an ELT Release Confirmation showing an ACTIVE lien: the examiner
stops processing and refers to Chapter 5. The lien cannot be resolved at the Marion counter.
The customer must contact the lienholder to obtain an electronic release in Verdana's system.

EMISSIONS PROGRAM

Verdana operates its own state emissions testing program for vehicles registered in
Verdana metro areas.

IMPORTANT: A Verdana emissions test certificate is NOT accepted by Marion DMV.
Marion requires a Marion-authorized emissions test regardless of any test performed
in Verdana. See Admin. Rule 2.4 for Marion emissions requirements.

(Note: Verdana having an emissions program is a Verdana matter — it does not affect
Marion's requirement. See also Admin. Rule 2.4 § 2.4.6.)

NOTE ON VERDANA "REBUILT":
Verdana's "Rebuilt" designation means the vehicle was rebuilt or restored per manufacturer
specification, using replacement components, but was NOT previously declared salvage.
This is equivalent to Marion's "Rebuilt" brand (MVC § 310(3)).

This is DIFFERENT from Halloway's "Rebuilt" designation, which means rebuilt from a salvage
condition (equivalent to Marion "Reconstructed"). The same word "Rebuilt" is used by
both Verdana and Halloway, but they mean different things. Always identify the origin
state before applying a brand equivalency. See Brand Equivalency Guide for full detail.

SALES TAX

Verdana state sales tax rate on vehicle transfers: 5.0%.

Marion has a reciprocity agreement with Verdana. Customers may receive a credit for
Verdana tax paid, as computed under Admin. Rule 7. See Marion Tax Reciprocity Schedule.
