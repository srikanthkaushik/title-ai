CHAPTER 4 — DOCUMENT CHECKLISTS
§ 4.3 — RELOCATION TRANSFER (PAPER OR ELT ORIGIN TITLE)

Document ID: PM-CH4-3 | Authority: Admin. Rules 2.1, 2.4, 7, 9 | Revision: 2024-03

                    ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
                    Examiner Procedure Manual | Chapter 4 — Checklists | §4.3 Relocation | Rev. 2024-03
                    ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

SCENARIO:   Transfer Type: RELOCATION
            Origin Title Form: PAPER or ELT
            Lien Status: NONE (lien at time of relocation is uncommon; if present, see Ch. 5)
            Brand: CLEAN (any brand triggers supervisor referral — see Chapter 5)

This section applies when an owner brings an out-of-state vehicle to Marion upon relocating
and has NOT purchased the vehicle. The vehicle was already owned and titled before the
move.

Key differences from purchase transfers:
    • No bill of sale is required (no purchase occurred).
    • Proof of Marion residency is required instead.
    • Tax is based on NADA Clean Retail value, NOT purchase price.
    • Form TR-1 Block C must indicate RELOCATION; Block D (purchase info) is left blank.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

4.3.1  REQUIRED DOCUMENTS — Customer must produce ALL of the following:

☐  1. ORIGIN TITLE (if paper state) or ELT RELEASE CONFIRMATION (if ELT state).
       For paper title states: original title in customer's name, no lien notation (or
       lien fully released — use same release standards as § 4.2 Item 8).
       For ELT states: printed or emailed ELT Release Confirmation showing customer's name,
       vehicle VIN, and lien status as NONE or RELEASED. See also § 4.4.

☐  2. PROOF OF MARION RESIDENCY — Any TWO of the following, showing the customer's
       Marion address:
       (a) Signed lease agreement or mortgage statement in customer's name at a Marion
           address;
       (b) Marion utility bill (electric, gas, water) in customer's name, dated within
           60 days of the application;
       (c) Marion employer pay stub or employer letter, on company letterhead, showing
           customer's Marion address, dated within 60 days.
       Residency must have been established within 90 days prior to the title application.
       Residency proof older than 90 days before application requires supervisor review.

☐  3. MARION FORM TR-1 — Out-of-State Title Transfer Application:
       • Block C: Check RELOCATION (do not check PURCHASE).
       • Block D: Leave BLANK (no purchase information).
       • Block F: Odometer disclosure if vehicle is ≤ 10 model years old; otherwise EXEMPT.
       • Block H: Lien = NONE (or RELEASED with documentation attached).
       • Customer signature and date in Block I.

☐  4. MARION FORM TR-2 — VIN Inspection Certification, completed by authorized inspector.
       Required for all out-of-state transfers, including relocation.
       Dated within 90 days of application.

☐  5. PROOF OF MARION INSURANCE — as specified in § 4.1.1 Item 5.

☐  6. PAYMENT — Due at time of application:
       • Title transfer fee: $25.00
       • VIN inspection fee: $15.00
       • Base registration fee: $45.00 (≤ 5,000 lbs) or $65.00 (5,001–8,500 lbs)
       • Use tax: 5.5% of NADA Clean Retail value, less any reciprocity credit.
         See § 4.3.2 for relocation tax note. Customer should bring documentation of
         tax paid in origin state (for reciprocity credit calculation).

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

4.3.2  TAX NOTE — RELOCATION TRANSFERS

For relocation transfers, taxable value is the NADA Clean Retail value of the vehicle
as of the date of application (not the customer's original purchase price paid years ago).

The examiner or customer looks up the NADA value using the Marion DMV terminal or a
printed NADA guide. The customer may bring a NADA printout; the examiner verifies it
is current.

Reciprocity credit for relocation: The credit still applies if the origin state has a
reciprocity agreement with Marion. Customer must provide documentation of tax paid in
the origin state. Note: for relocation transfers, "tax paid in origin state" typically
refers to sales or use tax paid when the vehicle was originally purchased in that state —
not a new transaction.

    Example: Customer relocated from Verdana. Verdana tax paid on original purchase:
    $800. NADA value of vehicle today: $14,000. Marion use tax = $14,000 × 5.5% = $770.
    Credit = min($800, $770) = $770. Additional due to Marion = $0.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

4.3.3  EMISSIONS TESTING

Same county/model year rule as § 4.1.2 (Admin. Rule 2.4). Registering county is the
county of the customer's Marion address. See § 4.1.2 for the full note.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

4.3.4  EXAMINER NOTES

• The 90-day residency window: the customer must have established Marion residency within
  90 days before the application. If residency was established more than 90 days ago,
  the customer may be subject to a late penalty (Admin. Rule 9). If residency proof
  is undated or unclear, refer to supervisor.

• Relocation from an ELT state: if the origin state is ELT (e.g., Verdana), the customer
  presents an ELT Release Confirmation instead of a paper title. See also § 4.4 for
  the ELT-specific process. Use both § 4.3 (relocation requirements) and § 4.4 (ELT
  procedure) together for relocation-from-ELT-state scenarios.
