CHAPTER 6 — FEE COMPUTATION WORKSHEET
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Complete the following steps in order for every out-of-state title transfer.
Reference: Admin. Rule 9 (current, effective 2023-01-01) and Admin. Rule 7.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

6.1  STEP 1 — TITLE TRANSFER FEE (always due)

$25.00 — Title transfer application fee (Form TR-1).
Applies to every out-of-state title transfer, purchase or relocation.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

6.2  STEP 2 — REGISTRATION FEE (always due)

Determine vehicle weight class from the title or VIN decode:
    ≤ 5,000 lbs GVWR (most passenger vehicles): $45.00
    5,001–8,500 lbs GVWR (large SUVs, light trucks): $65.00

Note: GVWR appears on the title or can be obtained from the VIN decode. If not available,
the examiner looks up the manufacturer's GVWR from the vehicle description.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

6.3  STEP 3 — VIN INSPECTION FEE (always due)

$15.00 — VIN inspection fee (Form TR-2).
Collected at the DMV window regardless of who performed the inspection.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

6.4  STEP 4 — EMISSIONS TEST FEE (conditional — paid to testing station, not DMV)

Determine if emissions testing is required:
    Required if: (a) registration county is Marion County, Riverside County, or Capital County
                 AND (b) vehicle model year is < 25 years old from current calendar year.

    If required: $35.00 — paid by customer to the authorized testing station.
    Marion DMV does NOT collect emissions fees. List on the customer's total for
    information, but note "Paid to testing station."

    If NOT required: $0 (enter "$0 — exempt" on worksheet and note the reason).

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

6.5  STEP 5 — SALES / USE TAX (always due; amount varies)

Computation (see Admin. Rule 7 for full detail):

    (a) Determine taxable value:
        PURCHASE: greater of declared price or NADA Clean Retail (if declared ≥ 80% of NADA,
                  use declared; if < 80% of NADA, use NADA).
        RELOCATION: NADA Clean Retail value only.

    (b) Marion tax = taxable value × 5.5%

    (c) Reciprocity credit:
        Check Tax Reciprocity Schedule for origin state.
        If YES: credit = min(tax paid in origin state, Marion tax owed).
        If NO: credit = $0.

    (d) Additional Marion tax owed = max(0, Marion tax − credit)
        If credit ≥ Marion tax: additional due = $0 (no refund).

    Enter the final additional tax owed on the worksheet.

    Common computations:
    — Verdana purchase, $20,000: Marion tax = $1,100; Verdana tax = $1,000 (5%);
      credit = $1,000; additional due = $100.
    — Crestwood purchase, $15,000: Marion tax = $825; Crestwood tax = $900 (6%);
      credit = $825; additional due = $0.
    — Pembrook purchase, any amount: credit = $0; full Marion tax owed.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

6.6  STEP 6 — LIEN RELEASE PROCESSING FEE (conditional)

$5.00 — Only if Form TR-3 (Lien Release Acknowledgment through Marion DMV lender system)
is used. No fee for lien releases by title endorsement or separate lienholder letter.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

6.7  TOTAL DUE TO MARION DMV

Sum of Steps 1 + 2 + 3 + 6 + tax owed (Step 5).
Step 4 (emissions, $35.00) is paid separately to the testing station, not to DMV.

Example total — Crestwood purchase, $15,000, Marion County, 2010 vehicle, ≤ 5,000 lbs:
    Step 1: $25.00
    Step 2: $45.00
    Step 3: $15.00
    Step 4: $35.00 (emissions — to testing station; vehicle is 16 years old < 25 yr threshold)
    Step 5: $0.00 (Crestwood reciprocity — credit $825 ≥ Marion tax $825)
    Step 6: $0.00 (no TR-3)
    TOTAL TO DMV: $85.00 + $35.00 to testing station

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

6.8  LATE PAYMENT PENALTY

If the customer is past the 30-day application deadline: 10% of total fees and tax owed
per month elapsed, capped at 50%. Determine elapsed months from purchase or residency date.

CHAPTER 7 — FORM CATALOG
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

7.1  FORM TR-1 — Out-of-State Title Transfer Application

Purpose: Primary application for all out-of-state title transfers.
Required for: ALL transfers (purchase and relocation).
Completed by: Customer (with examiner guidance as needed).
Key blocks: Transfer type (C); purchase info (D); lien status (H); customer signature (I).
See Form TR-1 Instructions for field-by-field guidance.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

7.2  FORM TR-2 — VIN Inspection Certification

Purpose: Documents the result of the mandatory VIN verification inspection.
Required for: ALL out-of-state transfers.
Completed by: Authorized inspector (NOT the customer; NOT the examiner).
Key blocks: Inspector credentials (A); VIN readings (B); certification (C); discrepancy notes (D).
Validity: 90 days from inspection date. Expired forms must be rejected.
See Form TR-2 Instructions.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

7.3  FORM TR-3 — Lien Release Acknowledgment

Purpose: Records a lien release processed through Marion DMV's registered lender system.
Required for: Lien releases submitted via Marion DMV lender portal only. NOT required for
              lien releases by title endorsement or separate lienholder letter.
Completed by: Lienholder (or DMV on lienholder's behalf). Not completed by customer.
Fee: $5.00 lien release processing fee when TR-3 is used.
See Form TR-3 Instructions.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

7.4  FORM TR-10 — Supervisor Exception Referral

Purpose: Documents an exception condition and routes it to the supervisor for review.
Required for: ANY condition listed in Chapter 5 (active lien, brand, missing docs, unrecognized state, VIN discrepancy).
Completed by: Examiner (Blocks 1–4); Supervisor (Block 5 disposition).
No fee to applicant.
See Form TR-10 Instructions.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

7.5  FORM EMIT-1 — Emissions Certificate

Purpose: Evidence that the vehicle passed a Marion-authorized emissions test.
Required for: Vehicles subject to emissions testing (metro county + model year < 25 yrs).
Completed by: Authorized testing station (issued to customer upon passing test).
Not a Marion DMV form — issued by the testing station, brought to DMV by customer.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

7.6  FORM OD-1 — Odometer Disclosure Statement

Purpose: Documents the disclosed odometer reading for vehicles ≤ 10 model years old.
Required for: Vehicles 10 model years old or newer, if odometer disclosure is not already
              on the origin title face or in TR-1 Block F.
Completed by: Customer (or contained in origin title assignment — many states include
              odometer disclosure in the title assignment section).
