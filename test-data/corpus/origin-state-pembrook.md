OVERVIEW

Pembrook uses a mixed title system: paper titles for most vehicles, with electronic
lien and title (PemELT) available for lienholder-participating lenders. Examiners
may receive either a paper title or a PemELT Release Confirmation from Pembrook
customers.

CRITICAL — TAX: Pembrook has NO sales tax reciprocity agreement with Marion. Full
Marion tax applies to all Pembrook transfers. See Tax section below.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

TITLE SYSTEM: MIXED (PAPER or ELT)

Pembrook paper titles:
• Physical title issued by the Pembrook Division of Motor Vehicles.
• Title number format: PMB-XXXXXXXX-YY.
• Assignment section on the reverse; standard paper title procedures apply.

Pembrook ELT (PemELT):
• Lienholder-participating banks and credit unions in Pembrook opt into PemELT.
• For vehicles financed through a PemELT-participating lender, no paper title is
  issued to the owner; the title record is held in PemELT.
• After lien release, the customer obtains a PemELT Release Confirmation from the
  Pembrook DMV portal.
• For Marion purposes, the PemELT Release Confirmation is treated the same as any
  ELT Release Confirmation. See Admin. Rule 2.1 § 2.1.3 and Procedure Manual § 4.4.

How to determine which system applies: Ask the customer whether they received a paper
title or whether their lender used PemELT. If the customer is unsure, ask for whatever
document they have from Pembrook DMV. The document type (paper title vs. PemELT Release
Confirmation) is self-evident from the document itself.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

LIEN PROCEDURES IN PEMBROOK

Paper title lien release: Lienholder endorsement on the title face. Standard Marion
requirements apply — see Procedure Manual § 4.2.

PemELT lien release: Electronic release submitted by the lienholder through the
Pembrook lender portal. Customer obtains a new PemELT Release Confirmation showing
lien status as RELEASED. Standard ELT lien release process applies — see § 4.4.5.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

EMISSIONS PROGRAM

Pembrook operates a state emissions testing program for vehicles registered in Pembrook's
designated urban counties.

IMPORTANT: A Pembrook emissions test certificate is NOT accepted by Marion DMV.
Marion requires a Marion-authorized emissions test for qualifying vehicles.
See Admin. Rule 2.4.

BRAND TERMINOLOGY IN PEMBROOK

Pembrook uses the following brand designations. Note especially "Salvage Rebuilt" —
it is a single compound Pembrook brand, not two separate brands.

┌──────────────────────────┬──────────────────────────┐
│ Pembrook Brand           │ Marion Equivalent         │
├──────────────────────────┼──────────────────────────┤
│ Junk                     │ Salvage                   │
│ Salvage Rebuilt          │ Rebuilt                   │
│ Water Damage             │ Flood Damage              │
│ Odometer                 │ Odometer Rollback         │
└──────────────────────────┴──────────────────────────┘

★★★ CRITICAL NOTE — PEMBROOK "SALVAGE REBUILT" IS ONE BRAND ★★★

Pembrook's "Salvage Rebuilt" is a SINGLE compound designation. It means a vehicle that
was declared salvage (Pembrook calls it "Junk") in Pembrook and has since been rebuilt
to roadworthy condition. Pembrook places BOTH descriptors in a single brand field:
"Salvage Rebuilt."

Marion equivalent: "Rebuilt" (one brand, not two).

Do NOT split this into two separate Marion brands ("Salvage" + "Rebuilt"). Pembrook's
"Salvage Rebuilt" means the vehicle IS NOW roadworthy and IS NOT currently in salvage
status — it has been rebuilt. Marion's "Rebuilt" (MVC § 310(3)) is the correct single
equivalent.

If you split it incorrectly into "Salvage" + "Rebuilt" on the Marion title, you would
be adding a brand (Salvage) that Pembrook did not apply to the vehicle in its current
state. The vehicle's current Pembrook status is "Salvage Rebuilt" — one concept.

Always consult the Marion Brand Equivalency Guide for authoritative mappings.

SALES TAX — ██ NO RECIPROCITY AGREEMENT ██

Pembrook DOES NOT have a sales tax reciprocity agreement with Marion.

This means: no credit is available for any Pembrook sales or use tax paid by the customer,
regardless of the amount. Full Marion tax at 5.5% of the taxable value is owed.

Example: Customer paid $1,500 in Pembrook tax on a $25,000 vehicle.
Marion tax = $25,000 × 5.5% = $1,375. Pembrook tax paid = $1,500.
Despite having paid more tax in Pembrook than Marion's rate, the customer owes $1,375
to Marion. No credit. No refund.

See Marion Tax Reciprocity Schedule (Pembrook row: NO AGREEMENT) and Admin. Rule 7 § 7.3.2.

FOOTER NOTE: Pembrook does not participate in Marion's tax reciprocity program.
No credit for Pembrook tax paid. See Tax Reciprocity Schedule.
