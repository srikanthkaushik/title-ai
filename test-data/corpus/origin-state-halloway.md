OVERVIEW

Halloway issues physical (paper) titles for all passenger motor vehicles. Customers from
Halloway will have a paper title to present. Halloway has been on Marion's recognized
states list (Admin. Rule 4) since 2012. NOTE: Halloway has a unique brand terminology
that differs from other states, particularly regarding the "Rebuilt" designation —
see the Brand section below and the Marion Brand Equivalency Guide.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

TITLE SYSTEM: PAPER

Halloway paper title characteristics:
• Physical title issued by the Halloway Motor Vehicle Commission.
• Title number format: HAL-YYNNNNNNN (year prefix followed by sequence number).
• Owner name, vehicle VIN, year, make, model on title face.
• Assignment section on the reverse; all assignments must be completed in ink.
• Odometer disclosure: Halloway requires odometer disclosure for vehicles up to
  12 model years old (note: this is Halloway's rule, not Marion's; Marion requires
  disclosure for vehicles ≤ 10 model years old).

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

LIEN PROCEDURES IN HALLOWAY

Halloway lien release: The lienholder places a NOTARIZED endorsement on the title face
when the lien is satisfied. Halloway does NOT issue separate lien release letters;
the notarized endorsement on the title face is the ONLY accepted form of lien release
for Halloway paper titles. An examiner should not accept a Halloway lienholder letter
in lieu of the title-face endorsement.

If an examiner receives a Halloway title with an unreleased lien and only a lienholder
letter (no title-face endorsement), refer to Chapter 5 — this does not satisfy Marion's
lien release requirement for Halloway titles.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

EMISSIONS PROGRAM

HALLOWAY DOES NOT OPERATE A STATE EMISSIONS TESTING PROGRAM. Vehicles registered in
Halloway are not required to pass an emissions test for Halloway registration purposes.
Halloway has no authorized testing stations and issues no emissions certificates.

IMPORTANT FOR MARION EXAMINERS: The fact that Halloway has no emissions program does
NOT mean that a vehicle from Halloway is exempt from Marion emissions testing.
Marion's emissions testing requirement (Admin. Rule 2.4) applies based on the Marion
registration county and the vehicle's model year — not on whether the origin state
has an emissions program. A Halloway vehicle registering in a Marion metro county
with a model year less than 25 years old is subject to Marion's emissions test.

See Admin. Rule 2.4 § 2.4.6: "Origin state emissions programs — Not accepted." That
section applies equally to Halloway (no program) and to Verdana (which has a program):
in both cases, Marion's requirement controls.

BRAND TERMINOLOGY IN HALLOWAY

Halloway uses the following brand designations. Note the critical distinction regarding
"Rebuilt" — this is a frequent source of error.

┌──────────────────────────┬──────────────────────────┐
│ Halloway Brand           │ Marion Equivalent         │
├──────────────────────────┼──────────────────────────┤
│ Rebuilt                  │ Reconstructed             │
│ Junk                     │ Salvage                   │
│ Flood                    │ Flood Damage              │
│ Odometer                 │ Odometer Rollback         │
└──────────────────────────┴──────────────────────────┘

★★★ CRITICAL NOTE — HALLOWAY "REBUILT" ≠ VERDANA "REBUILT" ★★★

In Halloway, the "Rebuilt" designation means a vehicle that was declared salvage (total
loss) in Halloway and has subsequently been rebuilt to a roadworthy condition.

This is the same CONCEPT as Marion's "Reconstructed" brand (MVC § 310(2) — salvage
vehicle rebuilt to roadworthy condition).

This is NOT the same as Verdana's "Rebuilt" designation. Verdana uses "Rebuilt" to mean
a vehicle rebuilt per manufacturer specification WITHOUT a prior salvage condition —
which is Marion's "Rebuilt" brand (MVC § 310(3)).

In short:
    Halloway "Rebuilt" → Marion "RECONSTRUCTED"   (rebuilt from salvage)
    Verdana  "Rebuilt" → Marion "REBUILT"          (rebuilt, no prior salvage)

Do NOT apply the Verdana "Rebuilt" rule when you have a Halloway title, or vice versa.
Always identify the origin state and look up the brand in the Marion Brand Equivalency Guide.
The Brand Equivalency Guide is the governing document; this profile is for reference only.

SALES TAX

Halloway state sales tax rate on vehicle transfers: 4.5%.

Marion has a reciprocity agreement with Halloway. Customers receive credit for Halloway
tax paid, up to the Marion tax owed. See Admin. Rule 7 and Marion Tax Reciprocity Schedule.

Example: Vehicle taxable value $20,000. Marion tax = $1,100. Halloway tax paid = $900
(4.5%). Credit = min($900, $1,100) = $900. Additional Marion tax due = $200.
