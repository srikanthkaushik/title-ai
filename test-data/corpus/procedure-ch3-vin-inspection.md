3.1  APPLICABILITY — ALL OUT-OF-STATE TRANSFERS

A VIN inspection is required for every out-of-state title transfer processed at Marion DMV,
without exception. This requirement applies regardless of:
    — vehicle age or model year
    — title brand status (clean or branded)
    — purchase price
    — whether the applicant is a PURCHASE or RELOCATION transfer
    — the origin state's title form (paper or ELT)

No examiner may waive or skip the VIN inspection requirement for any reason. Applications
received without a completed, valid Form TR-2 are incomplete and shall be rejected.

Legal authority: Marion Vehicle Code § 330(a); Admin. Rule 3.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

3.2  WHO MAY INSPECT

The VIN inspection must be performed by one of the following:

(a) A Marion-licensed Motor Vehicle Inspector. License numbers for authorized inspectors
    begin with the prefix "MVI-". The inspector's current license must be valid on the
    date the inspection is performed.

(b) An active, sworn Marion law enforcement officer (municipal, county, or state level).
    Badge or officer identification number must be recorded on Form TR-2 Block A.

(c) A licensed Marion motor vehicle dealer. Dealer license numbers begin with "DLR-".

Inspections performed by mechanics, body shops, garages, origin-state officials, or any
person not in category (a), (b), or (c) above are NOT accepted. The examiner shall reject
a Form TR-2 completed by an unauthorized inspector.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

3.3  WHAT IS INSPECTED

The authorized inspector physically examines the following VIN locations on the vehicle:

Primary location (required):
    Dashboard VIN plate — the metal or plastic plate affixed to the driver's side of
    the dashboard, visible through the windshield from outside the vehicle.

Secondary location (required):
    Driver-side door jamb sticker — the manufacturer's VIN label affixed to the door
    jamb or door frame.

Tertiary location (inspect if accessible without tools):
    Engine block — VIN stamped or affixed on the engine block. Inspector notes if
    inaccessible (engine components blocking access); this does not invalidate the
    inspection if the primary and secondary locations match.

All inspected VIN locations must match each other and must match the VIN on the origin
title or ELT Release Confirmation exactly, character for character.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

3.4  COMPLETING FORM TR-2

The authorized inspector completes Form TR-2 as follows:

    Block A: Inspector's printed name; license number (MVI-XXXXX), badge number, or
             dealer number (DLR-XXXXX); date of inspection.

    Block B: Vehicle VIN as read from each location inspected.
             — Dashboard plate VIN: _______________
             — Door jamb sticker VIN: _______________
             — Engine block VIN (if accessible): _______________
             If a location was inaccessible, mark "N/A — inaccessible."

    Block C: Inspector certification — inspector signs and dates, certifying that the VINs
             match the origin title and that the vehicle description matches the title.

    Block D: Discrepancy notes — if any VIN location does not match the title, describe
             the discrepancy here. Check the "DISCREPANCY" box in Block B.

The customer brings the completed Form TR-2 to the DMV office as part of the title
application package. The inspector does not submit the form to DMV directly.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

3.5  VIN DISCREPANCY — EXAMINER ACTION

If the completed Form TR-2 indicates a VIN discrepancy (Block D completed, "DISCREPANCY"
box checked, or VINs in Block B do not match the origin title), the examiner shall:

    (1) Stop processing the application.
    (2) Refer to Chapter 5 and complete Form TR-10, checking the "VIN discrepancy" box
        in Block 3.
    (3) Do not tell the applicant the nature of the discrepancy. Use the standard referral
        language from Chapter 5.

Examiners shall not attempt to independently determine whether a discrepancy is a
transcription error or represents a true VIN mismatch. That determination belongs to
the supervisor.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

3.6  FORM TR-2 VALIDITY PERIOD

A completed Form TR-2 is valid for 90 days from the date of inspection (Block A date).
Applications received more than 90 days after the TR-2 inspection date require a new
VIN inspection and a new TR-2. The examiner checks the TR-2 date against the application
date and rejects expired forms.

Fee: The VIN inspection fee of $15.00 is collected at the DMV window at the time of
application. This fee covers the DMV's processing cost and is not paid to the inspector.
