§ 2.4.1  APPLICABILITY

(Same as current version — unchanged by 2023 amendment.)

Emissions inspection requirements apply to all motor vehicles registering in Marion,
including vehicles transferred from out-of-state titles. The registration county and
vehicle model year determine whether an emissions test is required.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

§ 2.4.2  COUNTY CLASSIFICATION

(Same as current version — county classification unchanged by 2023 amendment.)

METRO COUNTIES (emissions testing required for qualifying vehicles):
    • Marion County
    • Riverside County
    • Capital County

RURAL COUNTIES (emissions testing NOT required — all vehicles exempt):
    All Marion counties not listed above.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

§ 2.4.3  VEHICLE AGE EXEMPTION   [SUPERSEDED — SEE CURRENT VERSION AR-2.4-CURRENT]

Under this version (effective July 1, 2018 through December 31, 2022): A motor vehicle
with a model year that is 20 or more years prior to the calendar year of registration is
exempt from emissions testing, regardless of county.

Calculation (prior rule): Vehicle is exempt if (Calendar Year of Registration) −
(Vehicle Model Year) ≥ 20.

Example under this prior rule: In calendar year 2022, vehicles with model year 2002
or earlier were exempt. A 2003 model year vehicle in 2022 had an age of 19 years and
was NOT exempt under this version.

THIS EXEMPTION HAS BEEN AMENDED. Effective January 1, 2023, the threshold is 25 years.
Do not apply the 20-year threshold to any registration occurring on or after January 1, 2023.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

§ 2.4.4  ADDITIONAL EXEMPTIONS

(Same as current version — unchanged.)

    (a) New vehicles — current model year or immediately prior model year;
    (b) Battery electric vehicles (BEV) and hydrogen fuel cell vehicles;
    (c) Motorcycles and mopeds with engine displacement under 50cc;
    (d) Farm vehicles used exclusively for agricultural purposes.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

§ 2.4.5  PROOF OF COMPLIANCE

(Same as current version — unchanged.)

A valid Marion Emissions Certificate (Form EMIT-1) issued by a Marion-authorized emissions
testing station, dated within 90 days of application, bearing the vehicle VIN, showing
a passing result.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

§ 2.4.6  NOTE ON ORIGIN-STATE PROGRAMS

This version of the rule does not explicitly address origin states without emissions
programs. Examiners should consult the current version (AR-2.4-CURRENT) and the
Examiner Procedure Manual for guidance on Halloway and similar states.
