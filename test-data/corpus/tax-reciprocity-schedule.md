INTRODUCTION

Marion has entered sales and use tax reciprocity agreements with certain states pursuant
to Marion Vehicle Code § 322. Where a valid reciprocity agreement exists, applicants
receive a credit against Marion vehicle transfer tax for sales or use tax verifiably paid
to the origin state, as computed under Admin. Rule 7 § 7.3.

For vehicles transferred from a state NOT listed in this schedule (or listed as having
NO AGREEMENT), no credit is available. Full Marion tax at 5.5% applies to the taxable
value regardless of any tax paid to the origin state.

Marion's current vehicle sales and use tax rate: 5.5% (effective January 1, 2023).

STATES NOT LISTED

States not appearing in the table above have NO reciprocity agreement with Marion.
Full Marion vehicle sales and use tax applies to transfers from any unlisted state.

This schedule reflects agreements in effect as of January 1, 2025. Examiners must use
the current version. Prior versions of this schedule may show different states or rates.

GENERAL COMPUTATION RULES (REFERENCE)

Credit formula:    credit = min(tax_paid_in_origin, marion_tax_owed)
Additional due:    max(0, marion_tax_owed − credit)
No refund:         If credit ≥ Marion tax owed, additional due = $0; Marion refunds nothing.
No reciprocity:    credit = $0; full Marion tax owed regardless of origin tax paid.

For full computation rules including taxable value determination, see Admin. Rule 7.
