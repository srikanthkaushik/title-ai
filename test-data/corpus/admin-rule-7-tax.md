§ 7.1  TAX RATE

Marion imposes a sales and use tax on all out-of-state vehicle transfers at the rate of
5.5 percent (5.5%) of the taxable value determined under § 7.2, effective January 1, 2023.

This rate applies to both purchase transfers (§ 7.2.1) and relocation transfers (§ 7.2.2).

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

§ 7.2  TAXABLE VALUE

§ 7.2.1  Purchase transfers

For vehicles acquired by purchase (including trade-in), taxable value is the greater of:
    (a) the declared purchase price as shown on the bill of sale or purchase agreement; or
    (b) the NADA Clean Retail value of the vehicle as published in the current edition of
        the NADA Official Used Car Guide (or approved successor guide) as of the date of
        the title transfer application.

Where the declared purchase price is at least 80 percent (80%) of the NADA Clean Retail
value, the examiner uses the declared purchase price. Where the declared purchase price
is below 80% of the NADA Clean Retail value, the examiner uses the NADA Clean Retail value.

Example: Declared price $12,000; NADA Clean Retail $16,000. Ratio = 75% < 80%.
Taxable value = $16,000 (NADA), not $12,000 (declared).

Example: Declared price $15,500; NADA Clean Retail $16,000. Ratio = 96.9% ≥ 80%.
Taxable value = $15,500 (declared price).

§ 7.2.2  Relocation transfers

For vehicles brought to Marion by a relocating owner (Marion Vehicle Code § 321), no
purchase transaction exists. Taxable value = NADA Clean Retail value as of the date of
the title application. The applicant's original purchase price (paid in the origin state,
at some prior time) is not used as the taxable value.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

§ 7.3  RECIPROCITY CREDIT

§ 7.3.1  States with reciprocity agreements

Where the origin state appears in the Marion Tax Reciprocity Schedule as having an
agreement with Marion, the applicant receives a credit as follows:

    Credit = min( tax verifiably paid to origin state, Marion tax owed on taxable value )

    Additional Marion tax owed = max( 0, Marion tax owed − Credit )

If the credit equals or exceeds the Marion tax owed, the additional amount due to Marion
is zero (0). Marion does not issue refunds for credits that exceed the Marion tax owed.

Verification of tax paid: The applicant must produce documentation of the amount paid —
typically the original bill of sale with the tax line itemized, or a tax receipt from
the origin jurisdiction. The examiner may not estimate or waive the verification requirement.

§ 7.3.2  States without reciprocity agreements

Where the origin state does NOT appear in the Marion Tax Reciprocity Schedule as having
an agreement, no credit is available. Full Marion tax at 5.5% of the taxable value is
owed, regardless of any sales or use tax the applicant paid to the origin state.

This applies even if the origin state's tax rate equals or exceeds Marion's rate.

§ 7.3.3  Worked examples

Example 1 — Crestwood (reciprocity YES, rate 6.0%):
    Declared purchase price: $15,000
    NADA Clean Retail: $15,500 (declared price is 96.8% of NADA — use declared price)
    Marion tax owed: $15,000 × 5.5% = $825.00
    Crestwood tax paid: $15,000 × 6.0% = $900.00
    Credit: min($900.00, $825.00) = $825.00
    Additional Marion tax due: $825.00 − $825.00 = $0.00

    The applicant owes no additional tax to Marion. Marion issues no refund of the $75
    difference between Crestwood tax paid and Marion tax owed.

Example 2 — Pembrook (reciprocity NO):
    Declared purchase price: $20,000; NADA $20,000 (use declared price — match)
    Marion tax owed: $20,000 × 5.5% = $1,100.00
    Pembrook tax paid: $20,000 × 6.0% = $1,200.00 (irrelevant — no reciprocity)
    Credit: $0.00 (no agreement)
    Additional Marion tax due: $1,100.00

    The applicant owes the full $1,100.00 to Marion regardless of the $1,200.00 paid
    to Pembrook.

Example 3 — Verdana (reciprocity YES, rate 5.0%):
    Purchase price: $20,000; NADA $20,000
    Marion tax owed: $20,000 × 5.5% = $1,100.00
    Verdana tax paid: $20,000 × 5.0% = $1,000.00
    Credit: min($1,000.00, $1,100.00) = $1,000.00
    Additional Marion tax due: $1,100.00 − $1,000.00 = $100.00

§ 7.4  PAYMENT

All Marion vehicle transfer tax is due and payable at the time of the title application.
Accepted payment methods at Marion DMV offices: certified check, money order, debit card.
Personal checks and credit cards are not accepted for tax payments. Cash accepted at
offices with cash-handling designation (see Marion DMV office locator).

Late payment penalty: 10% of tax owed per month, applied from the date payment was due,
capped at 50% of the original tax owed. See Admin. Rule 9 for full fee and penalty schedule.
