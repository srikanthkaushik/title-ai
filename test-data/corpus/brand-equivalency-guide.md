LEGAL AUTHORITY AND PREAMBLE

The following schedule establishes the equivalency between brand designations used by
other jurisdictions and the brand nomenclature of the State of Marion, as established
in Marion Vehicle Code §§ 310–312. Examiners shall apply the Marion brand shown in
this schedule to the Marion title, regardless of the terminology appearing on the origin
title. This schedule is binding on all Marion DMV examiners and supervisors.

Any brand designation appearing on an origin title that is NOT listed in this schedule
shall be treated as an UNRECOGNIZED brand. Unrecognized brands require supervisor referral
under Admin. Rule 5.2(b) before a Marion title may be issued.

This schedule is reviewed and updated annually by the Marion DMV Administrator. The
current version (effective January 1, 2025) supersedes the prior version dated
January 1, 2022. Examiners must use only the current version.

UNRECOGNIZED BRANDS — EXAMINER PROCEDURE

If a brand designation appearing on an origin title is NOT found in the table above:

1. Record the exact brand term as it appears on the title.
2. Do not attempt to assign a Marion equivalent independently.
3. Refer to supervisor per Admin. Rule 5.2(b) and Procedure Manual Chapter 5.
4. Check "Branded title — UNRECOGNIZED" on Form TR-10 Block 3.

The supervisor, in consultation with Marion DMV Legal if needed, will determine the
appropriate Marion equivalent or whether the transfer can proceed.

CROSS-REFERENCE TO MARION BRAND DEFINITIONS

For the Marion brand definitions referenced in this schedule, see Marion Vehicle Code § 310:
    § 310(1) — Salvage
    § 310(2) — Reconstructed (salvage-rebuilt; roadworthy)
    § 310(3) — Rebuilt (not from salvage; repaired/restored)
    § 310(4) — Flood Damage
    § 310(5) — Odometer Rollback
    § 310(6) — Lemon Law Buyback

The Halloway "Rebuilt" / Verdana "Rebuilt" distinction is the single most common source
of equivalency errors. When in doubt: consult this table, not the origin state's profile.
